The Polaris system

One signal chain, engineered as a single instrument.

A vertical music center flanked by a pair of floor-standing speakers. Source, decoding, amplification and sound are designed together, so every link locks precisely into the next.

  1. Where it begins

    High-fidelity source

    Lossless streaming, CD, or vinyl. A great source unlocks everything the system can do.

  2. Digital to analog

    Built-in DAC

    Converts digital signals into analog voltage with precision, defining resolution and the purity of the background.

  3. The soul of the system

    Vacuum-tube preamplifier

    Source switching, fine volume control and small-signal buffering, shaping the finesse of every note.

  4. Purer division

    Vacuum-tube active crossover

    Replaces the passive crossovers inside ordinary speakers with active tube crossovers for a cleaner signal.

  5. The source of power

    Multi-channel power amplifier

    Matched to the speakers in impedance and power: 600 W RMS into 8 Ω, with firm, controlled bass.

  6. The final voice

    Polaris speakers

    Rear-firing full-range drivers and a bottom-mounted subwoofer driver turn the signal into a panoramic soundstage.

Supported by a dedicated power supply and an isolation base. Optional turntable for analog playback.

Inside Polaris

Three things we will not compromise.

Osci-free isolation

Perfect isolation. No rack required.

Our proprietary Osci-free full-range isolation matrix combines a triple-damping structure with adaptive frequency compensation, removing mechanical vibration before it can touch the sound.

Each component rests on precision-machined spikes in the cups of the one below, so the system becomes its own isolation rack, revealing the airy overtones of strings and the faintest movement of a singer's breath.

<0.0025%Resonance distortion, lab measured
FSA panoramic sound

The warmth of tubes. The speed of solid state.

Polaris blends the richness of vacuum tubes with the precision of transistor circuitry. Rear-firing full-range drivers fill the room, and a bottom-mounted subwoofer driver delivers deep, controlled authority.

Together they form the FSA panoramic sound reproduction system, with Spatial Stealth Reflection™ extending the speakers' response from 19 Hz to 35 kHz.
